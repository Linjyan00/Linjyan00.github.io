# About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), supervised by [Prof. Changwen Chen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=w2HXPUUAAAAJ) and [Dr. Xin Jin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=byaSC-kAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). My recent work focuses on multimodal foundation models and remote sensing understanding. If you are interested in collaboration, feel free to contact me by email.

## Research Interests
